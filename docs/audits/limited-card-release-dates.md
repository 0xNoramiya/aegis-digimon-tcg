# Limited-card release eligibility

Verified October 14, 2026. The catalog's `LM` set combines several products. It cannot supply one release date or a historical format cutoff. `getLimitedCardAvailability` records explicit membership, first English retail release, and any later tournament eligibility date. Reprints do not reset a card's original release.

Historical pools use the English/global schedule already used by other sets. They do not model the Korean or Japanese regional release calendars. The beta catalog uses retail release dates; historical deck legality uses tournament eligibility where Bandai states a later date. Current all-card formats retain their existing policy.

## Primary evidence

- [BT15 product](https://world.digimoncard.com/products/pack/ver15/) gives February 16, 2024; its [card list](https://world.digimoncard.com/cards/?category=522020&search=true) includes all twenty LM-001–020 cards.
- [Special Limited Set product](https://world.digimoncard.com/products/goods/special-limited-set.php) gives December 13, 2024; its [card list](https://world.digimoncard.com/cards/?category=522201&search=true) includes LM-021–038 and a reprint of LM-020. The latter retains its earlier BT15 date.
- [BT21 product](https://world.digimoncard.com/products/pack/ver21/) and [card list](https://world.digimoncard.com/cards/?category=522028&search=true) establish LM-039–044's English release with BT21.
- [Billion Bullet product](https://world.digimoncard.com/products/goods/limited-pack-billion-bullet.php) gives October 2025; [Bandai's official European distributor Asmodee](https://www.asmodee.co.uk/products/bcl2790347-digimon-card-game-limited-card-pack-billion-bullet) specifies the publisher release date October 24. The [official card list](https://world.digimoncard.com/cards/?category=522202&search=true) supplies membership.
- [Another Knight product](https://world.digimoncard.com/products/goods/limited-lm-07.php) gives March 2026; [Asmodee](https://www.asmodee.co.uk/products/bcl2802415-digimon-card-game-limited-card-pack-another-knight) specifies the publisher release date March 27. The [official card list](https://world.digimoncard.com/cards/?category=522203&search=true) supplies membership. These precise distributor dates are used rather than inventing a day from Bandai's month-only listings.
- [Final Crest product](https://world.digimoncard.com/products/goods/limited-lm-08.php) distinguishes US shipping August 4, other-region retail August 28, and official tournament eligibility September 4. The [official card list](https://world.digimoncard.com/cards/?category=522204&search=true) identifies LM-057–062.
- [Distancia Cero product](https://world.digimoncard.com/products/goods/limited-lm-09.php) distinguishes US shipping November 2, other-region retail November 27, and tournament eligibility December 11. Its [new-card product image](https://world.digimoncard.com/images/products/goods/limited-lm-09/img_03.jpg?01) visibly identifies all six LM-063–068 cards. These dates are announced future dates and may change.

The worldwide retail convention uses the other-region release field, not US mail-order shipping. No Korean-calendar support is inferred from these dates. Future LM cards without metadata remain excluded from historical pools; they do not inherit another product's date.
