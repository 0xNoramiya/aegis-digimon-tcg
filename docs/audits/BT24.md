# BT24 targeted verification

## Scope

This ledger records targeted behavior verification, not a complete audit of every clause on the named card.

### BT24-034 — Aegiomon

The [official card image](https://world.digimoncard.com/images/cardlist/card/BT24-034.png) and committed catalog give Aegiomon Barrier both as a printed keyword and as an inherited keyword. The IR preserves both placements. Comprehensive Rules §16-25 restricts Barrier to preventing deletion in battle by trashing the top security card. It does not prevent removal by an effect merely because an attack is underway.

[Issue #5532](https://github.com/vinicius3333/aegis-digimon-tcg/issues/5532), replay report 251, reproduces all 64 inputs without divergence. Jupitermon: Wrath Mode has inherited Barrier from Aegiomon. Susanoomon EX12-076 redirects its attack with Raid, then resolves its When Attacking effect that places an opposing Digimon on top of security. This is effect-driven removal, before battle deletion. The offered prevention comes from Jupitermon BT24-101 through Succession, and the player accepts it at input 63. The resulting decision orders effects triggered by removing security; Barrier is correctly absent from that decision.

The [official Susanoomon image](https://world.digimoncard.com/images/cardlist/card/EX12-076.png) confirms the placement effect. Local Q7194 also identifies this When Attacking processing as a would-leave effect window. Local Aegiomon Q5613 and Q6713 concern Tamer play and do not change Barrier's timing.

`barrier-battle-versus-attack-effect.fixture.ts` and its scenario test verify both cases through public intents: Susanoomon's effect offers the inherited Jupitermon leave protection without Barrier, while an actual battle loss offers Barrier alongside that protection and allows the Digimon to survive. The attack and resulting effects are fully resolved before final assertions. Existing Barrier ordering, repeated-battle, and battle-entry suites also pass.

No rules or card implementation change is warranted for the reported position. No broader Barrier defect was established; this investigation does not claim a full audit of Aegiomon's other clauses or of all Barrier-bearing cards.

Validation: 10 tests passed across the new scenario and four existing combat suites. `pnpm typecheck`, the full `pnpm check`, and `git diff --check` passed, with existing repository lint warnings. No production behavior changed, and no separate browser run was made for this branch.
