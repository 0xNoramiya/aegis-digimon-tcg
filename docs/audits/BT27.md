### BT27-073 — Chiropmon

Catalog: `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json`, card `BT27-073`. Purple level 3, play 3, 2000 DP; purple level 2 evolution 0 and alternate Glowing Dawn level 2 evolution 0.

Printed contract: during your turn, reduce evolution into a purple BEATBREAK Digimon by 1. Inherited When Attacking, once per turn: draw 1, then trash 1 from hand.

Trace: `apps/api/src/cards/BT27/BT27-073.ts` registers compiled IR exclusively. `Replacement/wouldDigivolve` checks both color and trait. Inherited `Draw` precedes mandatory `Trash`.

Proof: `BT27-073.test.ts` covers evolution into both NightChiropmon printings, a non-BEATBREAK purple peer with no reduction, actual inherited attacks twice in one turn, and alternate egg evolution across colors with a nonmatching egg rejected.

KB: `node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT27-073` returned no entries. Printed English and Japanese clauses agree.

**10/10** (c + ir + b + p + g). No known printed-clause ambiguity.

### BT27-077 — NightChiropmon

Catalog: `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json`, card `BT27-077`. Purple level 4, play 4, 5000 DP; purple level 3 evolution 2 and alternate Glowing Dawn level 3 evolution 2.

Printed contract: Security and When Digivolving, with at most one own Tamer, optionally play a Tamer whose name contains Makoto Kuonji from hand for free. Inherited Retaliation.

Trace: `apps/api/src/cards/BT27/BT27-077.ts` registers compiled IR exclusively. Both recruitment timings use `PlayWithoutCost`, a Tamer/name filter and the board count limit. Retaliation is inherited only.

Proof: `BT27-077.test.ts` covers zero, one and two existing Tamers, an unrelated Tamer remaining in hand, refusal, an actual security attack and inherited Retaliation deleting the battle winner. Evolution is also exercised with BT27-073 below it and BT27-080 above it.

KB: `node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT27-077` returned no entries. Printed English and Japanese clauses agree.

**10/10** (c + ir + b + p + g). No known printed-clause ambiguity.

### BT27-080 — ScourgeChiropmon

Catalog: `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json`, card `BT27-080`. Purple level 5, play 7, 7000 DP; purple level 4 evolution 3 and alternate Glowing Dawn level 4 evolution 3.

Printed contract: Hand/Main with a Makoto Kuonji Tamer, place an exact NightChiropmon from trash at an exact Chiropmon's bottom to evolve into this card ignoring level, paying the evolution cost. On Play/When Digivolving delete an opposing level 4 or lower Digimon, then optionally trash a bottom face-down card under an own Tamer to gain Rush and Piercing for the turn. Inherited On Deletion optionally play a Glowing Dawn card costing at most 5 from trash for free.

Trace: `apps/api/src/cards/BT27/BT27-080.ts` registers compiled IR exclusively. `DigivolveViaPlacement` preserves bottom order and honors the level-only permission, color and the prohibition against ignoring requirements. `Delete` precedes the independent `CostGatedBlock`; both keywords are granted only after payment. Inherited `PlayWithoutCost` filters trait and play cost. Engine seam: [placement evolution with level-only ignoring](engine/placement-evolution-level-requirement.md).

Proof: `BT27-080.test.ts` covers both Chiropmon printings, source order, the BT27-073 discount, inherited Retaliation, missing Makoto, the BT8-059 prohibition, ordinary purple evolution cost and yellow rejection, level-4 deletion preserving level 5, payment/refusal/unavailable payment, a newly played Rush attack with a Piercing security check, turn-end expiration, revival of Digimon and Tamers including exact cost 5, and rejection of expensive or unrelated cards.

KB: `node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT27-080` returned no entries. Printed English and Japanese clauses agree.

**10/10** (c + ir + b + p + g). No known printed-clause ambiguity.

### BT27-082 — Nosferamon / Rampage Bullet

Catalog: `packages/shared/src/cards/data/cards.json`, card `BT27-082`. Purple/yellow level 6, 12000 DP, purple level 5 evolution 3, alternate Glowing Dawn level 5 evolution 3. Dual Option costs 5 and requires purple or a Glowing Dawn card. Undead/Glowing Dawn/BEATBREAK, Virus, Mega, SR.

Printed evidence: [English reveal](https://x.com/digimon_tcg_EN/status/2111698792240054544) and [Japanese reveal](https://x.com/digimon_tcg/status/2111698837064786432). Downloaded both attached sample images to `data/card-images/BT27-082.webp` and `BT27-082-J.webp`; Japanese art is registered in `arts.json`. Japanese transcription and provenance are recorded in the existing translation dictionaries and report.

Printed contract: Execute and Ascension. Security/On Deletion delete one lowest-level opposing Digimon; if no deletion occurs, trash their top security. When Digivolving/When Attacking share a once-per-turn activation: optionally pay two bottom face-down Tamer cards to activate Option Main. Rampage Bullet deletes one opposing level 6 or higher, then optionally revives a BEATBREAK Digimon or Tamer costing at most 4 from trash. Arts Digivolve allows free legal evolution after Option use.

Trace: `apps/api/src/cards/BT27/BT27-082.ts` registers compiled IR exclusively. `Delete` and conditional `SecurityManipulation` implement removal/fallback. `CostGatedBlock` and `ActivateMain` use a shared frequency key. `PlayWithoutCost` checks both trait and cost. Existing `WaiveColorRequirement`, keyword handlers and Arts Digivolve provide remaining behavior. No engine changes.

Proof: `BT27-082.test.ts` has 18 passing behavior cases: Security resolution, lowest-level removal, empty-board fallback, Ascension ordering canceling pending On Deletion when the card moves first, revival of Digimon and Tamers at cost 4, over-cost and unrelated rejection, ordinary purple and off-color trait evolution, retained sources/inherited Retaliation, off-color Option waiver and Arts Digivolve, unavailable payment, refusal, shared evolution/attack once-per-turn, direct-play prohibition and invalid color/evolution rejection. All BT27 tests plus related BT26-070/075 regressions pass (242 tests, 17 files). Application typecheck, repository style gate and effect snapshot check pass. Existing lint warnings remain; changed card files have no warnings. Translation source accounting now passes: the pre-existing untracked gaps across six BT27 cards are explicitly registered in both source reports. All 16 translation tooling tests pass; the new card has Japanese provenance and explicit Korean gaps.

KB: `node tools/kb/query.mjs card BT27-082` returned no entries. English and Japanese printed behavior agree. Independent review corrected the Japanese Ascension transcription to 天昇.

**9/10** (c + ir + b + p; g partial). No known implementation ambiguity. Card-specific coverage does not directly exercise deletion immunity, split payment across Tamers, or refusal of revival; those use existing shared mechanisms. Browser verification is recorded below when available.

Orca Browser verification: `.local/scenarios/nosferamon-evolution.fixture.ts` opened through `pnpm scenario open` using isolated ports 2582/2581/4182. Visible controls evolved the realistic Chiropmon/NightChiropmon/ScourgeChiropmon stack into Nosferamon for 3 memory, accepted the two-card Tamer payment and revived Makoto. Final observed state: memory 7, exact three evolution sources retained, opposing GranKuwagamon deleted, Tamer stack empty, Makoto in battle, no pending decision, presentation idle with zero pending steps, budget hits or expired gates. The local harness proxies artwork to the deployed mirror, so new images are downloaded locally but will be served there after normal asset deployment.
